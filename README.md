# SM PR Group / S&C Media

A one-page site for SM PR Group & S&C Media, a media studio specializing in clipping, short-form editing, and content promotion across TV, Animation, Music, Comedy, Influencers, and Brands.

## What's on the site, top to bottom

### 1. Header
The top bar on every page. Shows the "SM PR GROUP / S&C MEDIA" name, links to jump to each section (Overview, Case Studies, Core Services, Reel Insights, Contact), a "Start Campaign" button, and the office locations/email. On narrower screens, the links collapse into a menu button.

> **Planned change:** "Start Campaign" currently jumps down to the Contact section. It should instead route to the internal web app where employees clip videos, once that app exists and a URL is available.

### 2. Status Bar
The thin strip right under the header. Shows a live-looking "active campaign" indicator, the office locations, the service categories (TV, Animation, Music, Comedy, Influencers, Brands), and the total views stat.

### 3. Hero
The main introduction at the top of the page. Headline, a short pitch on what the studio does, and two buttons — "Explore Past Campaigns" (jumps to Case Studies) and "Download Pitch Deck (PDF)" (opens a popup to request the deck). Alongside it, a stat card shows total campaign views, a views breakdown, and a small trend chart.

### 4. Campaign Ticker
A scrolling strip of client and campaign names (The Voice, Doc (Hulu), MrBeast Campaigns, GoPuff × Druski × Tom Brady, Teddy Swims, Myles Smith, Zootopia, Citadel, Higgsfield AI, Dead Man's Wire) that continuously slides across the screen. Purely for credibility — showing the range of past work.

### 5. Master Case Studies
Three in-depth writeups of specific campaigns, each with a photo, a short strategy summary, and the results:
1. **The Voice — Performance Highlight Initiative**
2. **GoPuff × Druski × Tom Brady — Viral Comedy Series**
3. **Doc (Hulu) & Citadel — Suspense & Drama Campaigns**

Each one lists the specific numbers achieved (views, accounts reached, follows gained, etc.).

### 6. Reel Insights
A table of audited results across campaigns/titles — Doc (Hulu), The Voice, Dawson's Creek, Merrily We Go Along, MrBeast Campaigns, and Teddy Swims & Myles Smith — showing views, accounts reached, average watch time, follows gained, and skip rate for each. Below the table, four smaller cards call out extra wins: Zootopia, Higgsfield AI, Universal Basic Guys, and Kalshi.

### 7. Core Services
A grid of the 10 services offered:
1. Instagram Advertisements
2. Account Growth & Audience Reach
3. Caption Writing + Hashtag Strategy
4. Content Scheduling & Distribution
5. Applications of Meta's CRM
6. Video Editing & Audio Cleaning
7. Graphics Design
8. Website Design & Development
9. Aesthetic Feed Layouts & Story Curation
10. Content Calendar & Trend Strategy (with its own "Deploy Strategy Protocol" button, which leads to Contact)

### 8. Contact / Campaign Brief
The closing pitch — "Ready to Drive Viral Visibility Across Your Content?" — with location, turnaround time, and proof points on one side, and a form to submit a campaign brief (brand/artist name, email, and campaign objective) on the other.

### 9. Pitch Deck Popup
Opened from the hero's "Download Pitch Deck" button. Asks for a company email and industry, then confirms the deck is on its way.

### 10. Footer
Brand blurb, the same section links, office locations, contact email and reach stat, and the bottom bar with copyright and legal links (Clipping Rights, Terms of Service, Privacy Policy).

## Known Limitations / Next Steps

- **"Start Campaign" should route to the internal clipping web app.** All "Start Campaign" buttons currently just jump to the Contact section. Once the internal web app that lets employees clip videos exists, these should link out to it instead.
- **Forms don't actually send anywhere yet.** Submitting the campaign brief form or the pitch deck popup just shows a confirmation message — no email or request is actually sent out. This needs to be connected before going live.
- **No visitor analytics.** There's currently no way to see how many people visit the site or what they click on.
